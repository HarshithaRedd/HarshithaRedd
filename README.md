## Where data meets decision

The most useful analytics work rarely begins with a perfectly prepared dataset.

It usually begins with a question from leadership, two reports that do not agree, a spreadsheet someone updates every Monday, or important information spread across systems that were never designed to work together.

That is the part of the work I enjoy.

I bring those pieces together, identify what can be trusted, and turn them into dashboards, forecasts, analytical models, and automated workflows that help management make informed decisions.

My work sits at the intersection of **data and decision-making**. I care about the technical side—SQL, Python, data modeling, APIs, automation—but I also care about whether the final result is understandable, useful, and connected to a real action.

I use AI in the same way: not as decoration, but as another tool for reducing repetitive work, organizing unstructured information, explaining results, and helping people reach useful answers faster.

> **My goal is simple: turn scattered information into clear direction.**
